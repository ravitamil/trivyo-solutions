Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Trivyo Solutions

Trivyo Solutions ("we", "our", or "us"), founded by Ravikumar Tamilmani, is an independent software development and quality assurance studio. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to personal data when you visit our website (https://trivyo.in) or engage our services.

The Short Version

We do not track you. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or sell data. We only collect the information you choose to send us via email to discuss or deliver software and QA consulting services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide to us, including:

  • Direct Inquiries: Your name, email address, company name, and details about your engineering project when you email us at contact@trivyo.in.
  • Client Deliverables: Information necessary to fulfill a consulting statement of work (e.g., repository access, staging environment credentials provided under NDA).

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party marketing cookies.

We do not profile visitors across the web. You can browse our website without having any tracking code loaded onto your browser.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected communication exclusively to:

  • Respond directly to your project inquiries and consulting proposals.
  • Execute agreed-upon software engineering, QA automation, or AI agent development services.
  • Comply with legal, invoicing, and tax obligations under applicable Indian and international law.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share necessary transactional data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate our communications and website:

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (serves static website files).
  • Email Routing: ImprovMX (forwards incoming emails directly to our secure inbox).

5. Your Rights

Regardless of your location, you have the right to request access to the information you have provided to us, request correction, or request deletion of your correspondence at any time. Simply email us at contact@trivyo.in.
