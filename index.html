Before we
get started.

A few things you might want to know.

Can we start with just an idea?+

Yes. Describe the problem, who the software is for, and the outcome you want. We can help turn that into a scope and identify a sensible first version.

Can you work with our existing software?+

Yes. We start by reviewing the existing application, its integrations, and your priorities. That review helps us agree on the work and identify any constraints.

How do you decide where AI is useful?+

We look at the task, the data available, and what happens when an output is wrong. A pilot lets us evaluate the results and agree on human review before expanding the workflow.

How are cost and timelines agreed?+

We discuss requirements and dependencies first, then agree on scope, milestones, and fees in writing. Changes to the scope are discussed as the project develops.

Who owns the completed work?+

As set out in our terms, custom deliverables transfer to you upon full payment. Third-party components retain their own licences. Your project agreement defines the handover and any ongoing support.
